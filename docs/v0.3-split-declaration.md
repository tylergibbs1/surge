# v0.3 evaluation-lane declaration

This document freezes the v0.3 lane boundaries **before** any 2026 row exists in
the data store. It is written on 2026-08-02, when the newest observation on hand
is 2025-12-31T23:00Z.

Amending it is legitimate only while that remains true. Once 2026 data is
ingested, changing these boundaries is choosing a test set after seeing it.

## Why v0.2's lanes cannot simply continue

`docs/model-selection-experiment.md` froze v0.2 as: train before 2024,
validation 2024, locked test 2025 and later. That boundary is a calendar
constant, so it does not age.

v0.2's single authorized look at its locked lane was consumed on
2026-08-02T04:03:55Z by a fail-closed target-completeness failure, and no retry
is permitted (`artifacts/v0.2/surge-locked-test-receipt.json`, status `started`,
consuming). The lane is therefore burned: no metric can ever be published from
it.

Combined, those two facts mean every day of 2026 is born into a lane that can
never yield a claim. Today there is no legitimate evaluation lane for new data
at all. That is what this declaration fixes.

## Frozen boundaries

| Lane | Years |
|---|---|
| Train | before 2025 |
| Validation | 2025 |
| Locked test | 2026 and later |

Encoded as `surge.features.splits.V0_3`, declared but **not active**. The active
declaration remains `V0_2` so that every artifact already published keeps its
meaning. Activation requires the checklist below.

## Required disclosure: 2025 is not pristine

The consumed v0.2 run began model inference on 2025 origins before aborting on
the NYIS completeness assertion. No forecast metric was ever computed, printed,
or stored — the run failed inside the bootstrap's completeness guard, before
scoring — so no 2025 outcome was ever observed by a human or a selection rule.

The honest position is therefore: 2025 carries a recorded partial-inference
event with zero observed metrics. That is weaker than a never-touched holdout
and stronger than a tuned-on set. Any v0.3 result reported on 2025 must carry
this disclosure and must be labelled validation, never a locked test.

## Activation checklist

All of these must hold before `ACTIVE` moves to `V0_3`:

- [ ] 2026 load and weather are backfilled from January 2026 to the present, and
      the hourly Modal ingest is running so the gap cannot silently reopen.
- [ ] 2025 rows are re-fetched and compared against the current EIA vintage; the
      diff is recorded. The rows on hand were fetched 2026-05-10 and EIA revises.
- [ ] A new data snapshot is built and its manifest SHA-256 recorded. The v0.2
      snapshot `77d80d40…` is retained unchanged as the v0.2 record.
- [ ] The predeclared v0.3 questions below are committed before any 2026 metric
      is computed.
- [ ] `tests/test_splits.py` is updated in the same commit that moves `ACTIVE`,
      so the change cannot pass silently.

## Predeclared questions

Committed before the data exists, so they cannot be chosen to flatter a result.

1. **ISNE calibration drift.** Per-RTO interval coverage is stable in 2021–2022
   and drifts in 2023–2024, worst for ISNE (0.822 in 2023). Does the drift
   persist into 2025 and 2026? Success is a stated direction, not a threshold:
   the finding is reported whichever way it goes.
2. **Does adaptive calibration hold up out of sample?** Per-series ACI clears
   the ±2-point per-RTO bar on 2021, 2022 and the 2024 reporting half, and
   misses on 2023 (`docs/adaptive-conformal-experiment.md`). Run the frozen
   policy once on 2025. No selection step, no grid.
3. **Is the locked lane worth opening at all?** v0.3 must not open its 2026
   locked lane until a live-forward ledger exists, because a retrospective look
   at 2026 competes with the live-forward record for the same period and the
   live-forward record is the stronger evidence.

## What this does not change

No serving behaviour, no published claim, and no v0.2 artifact. `V0_3` is inert
until the checklist above is satisfied and `ACTIVE` is deliberately moved.
